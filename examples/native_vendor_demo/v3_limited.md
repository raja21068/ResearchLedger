# Demonstration manuscript — version 3

This is a **synthetic example**, not a human study or measured intervention.

## Methods
A single hypothetical convenience cohort of 80 participants received intervention X. There was no randomized concurrent control group and the participants were not randomly sampled.

## Results
The illustrative mean Y was 50 before and 60 after (20% relative difference). This is an arithmetic illustration, not an estimate of intervention effectiveness. Confidence intervals, source datasets, and independent replication were not provided.

## Conclusion
The observed difference is descriptive and limited to the measured cohort. This single-group example does not support a causal or population-general claim.
