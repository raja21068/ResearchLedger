# Evidence: E001

## Claim
The proposed aggregation rule improves accuracy over FedAvg.

## Experimental Context
10-client non-IID split, proposed method trained 200 rounds, FedAvg baseline trained 80 rounds
(round counts not matched between arms).

## Result
- Per-client accuracy ranges from 81.3% to 97.0% (client 3 is the 97.0% outlier).
- Mean accuracy across all 10 clients: 89.1%.
- FedAvg baseline mean accuracy (80 rounds): 84.6%.

## Observation
The manuscript's Results section reports "97% accuracy across all clients," which is the single
best client's result, not the across-client mean this evidence record actually supports (89.1%).
The two training runs also differ in communication-round budget (200 vs 80), which this record
does not control for — a fair FedAvg-at-200-rounds comparison has not been run.
